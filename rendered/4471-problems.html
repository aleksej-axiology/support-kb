parser: day-first dates read month first

The shared parser reads every date month first. A tenant whose locale writes the day first gets its dates back with day and month swapped: “7 April” goes out as “4 July”. This is the bug the #4471 page describes, and the fix queued there is for this one.

hint: booking-v3 asks for the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People booking a day-first tenant through that form type what the hint tells them. The symptom is the same swap, so these reports read like parser, but the parser fix does not reach them: the form’s hint has to follow the tenant’s locale. Only reports that came in through a booking-v3 tenant’s form channel have this cause.

one-day-move: the date shifts by a day

These are not swaps. “Tuesday 12 May” arrives as “Monday 11 May”, a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday while one in Toronto gets Monday, an invite arrives before the booking was made. The day moves and the month stays, which points at time zones, not at the parser. The desk already read these reports this way (desk-notes.json); the #4471 table still counts them under parser.

How the reports were split

  • One report per id. The export repeats some reports where its pages overlap.
  • The cause comes from the tenant: its locale, its booking form, and the channel the report came in by (causes.ts). Intake’s tag is not used: intake tags hint whenever the reporter’s browser is en-US, whatever the tenant runs.
  • Reports the desk noted as a one-day move are taken out of their cause and counted on their own (problems.ts).

Lessons

  • A subject line says what someone saw, not why. “Month and day swapped?” turns up under every problem here, one-day moves included, so matching on subjects cannot keep them apart.
  • Count a merged ticket by cause before marking it known. One fix per ticket is a claim the reports can check.
